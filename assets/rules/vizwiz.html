VIZWIZ

Show your data visualization skills! Compete to design dashboards and present insights with creativity and clarity.

Event Flow

  • All teams will participate in the Dashboard Creation & Presentation round.
  • A preliminary quiz on data visualization basics may be used to shortlist teams if participation is high.

Round Detail

  • Teams will have 60 minutes to create charts and an interactive dashboard from a provided dataset.
  • Each team will then deliver a short presentation.

Event Rules

  • Teams are limited to two participants.
  • The dataset and problem statement will be provided.
  • External resources, internet access and mobile phone use are prohibited during the event.
  • All visualizations, dashboards and content must be created during the allotted time.
  • Teams must submit a visual report, late submissions will be penalized.
  • Judges' decisions are final.
