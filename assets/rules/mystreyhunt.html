Mystery Hunt: The Grand Pursuit

Welcome, investigators! Your keen eyes and sharp minds will be put to the ultimate test. Below are the laws of the hunt. Decipher them, for they are your first clue to victory.

The Teams

  • Form your detective duo! Maximum 2 members per team.
  • Choose your partner wisely, for collaboration is key to unlocking the secrets that lie ahead.

Round 1: The Digital Eye – Visual Intelligence Test

  • 5 enigmatic images will be presented one by one.
  • Using Google Search (by keyword or reverse image), locate the exact same image and provide the source URL.
  • No automated tools, scanners, or bots are allowed.
  • Top 5 fastest and most accurate teams advance to Round 2.

Round 2: The Trail of Secrets – Physical Treasure Hunt

  • Each clue solved will lead to the next hidden object.
  • Clues must be solved in order – skipping is not allowed.
  • Each team has unique hidden clues to avoid overlap.
  • If you find another team's clue, leave it untouched.
  • Return moved objects (like books or pots) to their original place.
  • The first team to retrieve the final treasure wins.
