The Hidden Harmony

BGM Finder – Feel the Music. Crack the Clues.

Event Description

  • A thrilling music-based quiz testing knowledge across Tamil, English, and Hindi tracks.
  • Duration: Approx. 1.5 hrs
  • Team Size: Exactly 2 members
  • Prizes for Winner & Runner-up

Event Rounds

  • Round 1 – Paper & Pen Quiz: 20-min test on Tamil, English & Hindi music. Negative marking included.
  • Round 2 – Guess the Dialogue: Identify the movie/actor from famous dialogues. (Buzzer Round)
  • Round 3 – Lyric Finder: Identify the song from a displayed Tamil lyric line. (Buzzer Round)
  • Round 4 – Track Infos: Answer questions (singer, composer, movie) based on a played track. (Buzzer Round)

Rules

  • Maximum 2 participants per team.
  • Mobile phones and smart gadgets are strictly prohibited.
  • Any malpractice will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • In case of a tie, winners will be decided based on completion time.
