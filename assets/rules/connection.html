🧩 Connect-Crack

A thrilling multi-round quiz event testing accuracy, speed, and quick thinking!

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each team must consist of exactly 2 members.
  • Points will be awarded based on accuracy and speed.
  • In case of a tie, tie-breaker questions or time taken will decide.
  • Malpractice will lead to disqualification.
  • The Quizmaster’s decision is final.
  • Prizes for Winner and Runner-up teams.

Round 1: Preliminary Quiz

  • Each team gets only one attempt.
  • Each question has a 10-second timer.
  • Tie-break based on fastest response.
  • Points for correct answers & speed.
  • Top scoring teams qualify for next round.

Round 2: Connections

  • Each question will show a set of clues.
  • Teams must find the common link between clues.
  • Top teams move to the final round.

Round 3: Rapid Fire

  • Fast-paced buzzer round.
  • Answer immediately after buzzing.
  • Points for correct answers within time.
  • Quizmaster’s decision on buzzer, correctness & scoring is final.
